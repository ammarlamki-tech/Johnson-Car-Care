About us

Three decades of hands-on protection work in Muscat.

Johnson Car Care — also known for Johnson Window Films — has been protecting vehicles in Muscat since 1996. We work on paint, glass and interiors, using film and coating systems chosen for how they perform in Gulf conditions: sustained heat, intense UV, and fine airborne sand.

Alongside automotive work, we fit solar-control window film for homes and offices across Muscat, helping reduce the heat load on air conditioning through the same film technology we trust on cars.

FAQ

Common questions

Paint protection film is a physical layer that absorbs impacts like stone chips. Ceramic or graphene coating is a chemical sealant that adds gloss and makes the surface easier to clean. Many owners run both together — film where impact risk is highest, coating over the rest of the car.

Quality PPF is built to perform for years, not months, even under Muscat's heat and sun. Ask us about the specific film options and warranty terms for your vehicle when you book.

Heat-rejecting film blocks a significant share of infrared and UV energy before it reaches the cabin, which takes pressure off your air-con. The exact reduction depends on the film grade you choose — we'll talk you through the options.
